> THE LAST QUERY · RULESTHE STORY

> 11:45 PM, A STORMY NIGHTThe Story

Sir Victor Ravenscroft, 70, is found dead in his armchair, in the Library of his manor, next to a cold cup of tea.

His house is a smart house. Its AI, W.A.T.S.O.N., keeps a file on each of the 12 guests: age, height, shoe size, which hand they write with… The storm has cut the bridge. The killer is still inside, and has left traces all over the manor. Some of them are fake clues, planted to trick you.

> THE GAME IN 30 SECONDS

Explore the manor, collect clues, and use your phone to search the guest database. The first detective to name the killer wins.

  1. Every room has a deck of clue cards. Walk there to draw them.
  2. Some clues describe the killer. Some are dead ends. A few are fakes.
  3. On your phone, filter the 12 suspects with your clues. Each search costs 1 Query Token .
  4. Recharge your tokens in the Server Room, then accuse in the Library.

No SQL needed: the phone shows simple sentences… and the real SQL under each result.

> SETUP (3 MINUTES)

  1. The host starts a game in the Police Console app. Everyone joins with their phone.
  2. Shuffle each room deck (the room is on the back) and put it face down on its room.
  3. Everyone puts their pawn in the Grand Hall and takes 3 Query Tokens and a notebook sheet.
  4. The youngest player starts. Play goes clockwise.
03
> THE LAST QUERY · RULESYOUR TURN

> MOVE, ACT, QUERYYour Turn

1. Move

Move your pawn up to 2 rooms through the doors. You may also stay.

2. Act: one of these

Search: draw the top card of the room deck. Scan it (or read it) secretly and keep it face down in front of you. An empty deck means the room has been searched clean.

Recharge (Server Room only): refill up to 3 tokens. You can never have more than 3.

Accuse (Library only): see page 7.

Anytime: Query

During your turn, pay 1 to run one search on your phone, with up to 2 conditions. You can query several times if you have the tokens.

> THE SERVER ROOM

It is in the corner opposite the Library and has no cards. Going there to recharge costs you a search: choose the right moment!

04
> THE LAST QUERY · RULESTHE CLUES

> 40 CARDSThe Clues

Evidence cards all look the same: an object, its room and a QR code. Scan one to see what it reveals in this case. Behind it there can be:

True clueA real trait of the killer. Each one is hidden in 3 different rooms.
Dead endTrue, but useless to find the killer.
Fake cluePlanted by the killer. Only 1 copy, and it contradicts the real clues.

Evidence × 34

Scan it secretly in the Police Console, then keep it face down. Write what you learn in your notebook.

Bonus × 6

Printed, never scanned. Spare Battery: +1 . Running Shoes: move again. Master Key: peek at the top card of any deck.

> GOLDEN RULES

“Find the same clue twice, and it’s probably true.”
“0 suspects found? One of your clues is fake!”
05
> THE LAST QUERY · RULESTHE PHONE

> NO SQL NEEDEDThe Police Console

The query screen is a sentence with blanks. Fill them with the menus, then run it for 1 .

FIND guests WHERE [shoe size] is [44]
      AND [height] between [1.85] and [1.90]
→ 2 suspects found: Oscar Lindqvist, Hugo Ravenscroft
SQL: SELECT name FROM guests WHERE shoe = 44 AND height BETWEEN 1.85 AND 1.90;

You only see the names that match, never the whole table. That is why one query is not enough: with 3 or 4 clues you need several queries and your notebook to cross-check.

> 0 SUSPECTS FOUND

Nobody matches both conditions: one of your two clues is fake. Try them separately to find which one.

> W.A.T.S.O.N. UPDATES

Every 4 rounds (rounds 4, 8 and 12) the host taps End round and the app:

  • reveals one true trait of the killer to everyone;
  • gives every detective +1 (max 3).

> THE LAST QUERY · RULESACCUSE

> HOW TO WINAccuse

  1. Go to the Library. Accusing is your action.
  2. Say the killer’s full profile out loud, in English:
“The killer is ___: 47 years old, 1.86 m tall, size 44 shoes, left-handed!”
  1. Type the name in the app.
> RIGHT

You win! Read the solution in the app together.

> WRONG

Go back to the Grand Hall and lose all your . You stay in the game.

> TABLE TALK (SAY IT IN ENGLISH)

“Size 44 again! That’s the second time.”
“Zero suspects found… one of my clues must be fake.”
“I’m going to the Server Room to recharge.”

> WHAT YOU LEARNED

SELECT · WHERE · AND · BETWEEN · an empty result means a false condition.
